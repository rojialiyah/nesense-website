A low-cost, AI-assisted point-of-care system for early neonatal sepsis detection. Built for real healthcare settings, designed to save newborn lives.

Discover How It Works

The diagnostic window is small. The delay is deadly.

Globally, over 3 million neonates are affected by sepsis each year. In resource-constrained settings, clinical teams often wait 48 to 72 hours for blood culture confirmations.

We believe healthcare workers shouldn't have to wait days to make life-saving decisions.

For Zainab, the warning came too late.

The tragedy of Zainab's baby was not that sepsis had no treatment. The tragedy was that the early warning signs were not detected in time. NeoSense gives clinical teams the early signal that Zainab's baby did not have.

Clearer Decisions. Faster Action.

🩸

1. Detect

A low-cost lateral-flow strip detects infection-related biomarkers (like CRP) from a small sample in minutes.

📱

2. Interpret

Our mobile app captures the strip and utilizes AI computer vision to quantify line intensity, eliminating human visual error.

⚡

3. Recommend

Biomarker data and clinical inputs are combined to output a specific risk category and a recommended next clinical step.

Built for Real-World Resilience

  • ✅ Offline-Ready: Designed to function in rural Primary Healthcare Centres with low connectivity.
  • ✅ Highly Affordable: A strip-first model targeting under $2 per test to ensure sustainable impact.
  • ✅ Reduced NICU Burden: Automated interpretation supports busy nurses with clear escalation pathways.
